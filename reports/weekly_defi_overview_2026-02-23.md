## Summary & Key Narratives

1. **Risk-off week** — Broad TVL declines, volume down 20-40% across chains. This is a de-risking week.

2. **USDC dominance over USDT** — USDC minted +$801M while USDT saw -$154M in redemptions. The USDC/USDT ratio continues to shift. Institutional preference for USDC is strengthening.

3. **Ethereum as safe haven** — Nearly flat TVL (-0.1%) while every other major chain bled 5-10%+. $758M of stablecoin inflows confirm capital is consolidating on Ethereum.

4. **Uniswap V4 migration is THE story** — $5.6B of TVL migrated to V4 in one week. This is liquidity moving from V3 -> V4, not new capital. But it signals V4 is now the standard.

5. **Bitcoin DeFi retreat** — Babylon Protocol losing $743M (-18.5%) is the biggest single-protocol outflow. The BTC restaking thesis is being stress-tested.

6. **Solana paradox** — TVL down 9.2% but stablecoins up +$250M. SOL price decline drives TVL down, but stablecoin inflows suggest dry powder is being positioned.

7. **Lending/restaking rotation out** — Aave V3 (-$984M), Lido (-$859M), EigenCloud (-$477M), ether.fi (-$373M), Morpho (-$275M). Blue-chip DeFi protocols seeing meaningful outflows.

8. **RWA/institutional rails growing** — BlackRock BUIDL (+$100M), USYC (+$94M), YLDS (+$156M), Figure Markets (+$376M). The institutional pipeline keeps building regardless of market conditions.

9. **Basis trade unwind** — Resolv (-44%), Ethena USDe contraction (-2.5%). Funding rates compressing = basis trade less attractive.
