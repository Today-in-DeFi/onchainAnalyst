## Summary & Key Narratives

1. **Broad risk-off intensifies** — Last week was a de-risking week. This week is worse. Every major chain except Mantle and Provenance bled 2-19%. The aggregate absolute dollar outflows from top losers dwarf anything we've seen in recent weeks. This is a genuine market drawdown, not just rotation.

2. **Ethereum lost its safe-haven status** — The biggest shift from last week. ETH went from -0.1% to -5.95%, losing $3.3B in TVL. Stablecoin flows flipped from +$758M inflows to -$270M outflows. Whatever institutional bid was supporting ETH last week evaporated. ETH price action is now dragging TVL directly.

3. **USDC dominance accelerates** — +$1.04B minted for the second straight week while USDT contracts -$171M. This is becoming a structural shift. USDC market share is climbing and the institutional preference is clear. USDT's -$171M looks small but it's the consistency that matters — two consecutive weeks of net redemptions.

4. **Flight to gold, not stables** — The signal this week: Tether Gold (+$161M, +4.5%), Paxos Gold (+$108M, +4.7%). These are the biggest DeFi-native gainers by absolute dollars outside of Figure Markets. When risk-off money flows to gold-backed tokens instead of yield-bearing stables, that's a genuine fear signal.

5. **Babylon Protocol in freefall** — -$883M (-22%) this week after -$743M (-18.5%) last week. Cumulative damage is catastrophic. Bitcoin DeFi's flagship restaking protocol has lost over $1.6B in two weeks. The BTC restaking thesis is being actively rejected by the market.

6. **Blue-chip DeFi destruction** — Lido (-$1.5B), Aave V3 (-$1.5B), EigenCloud (-$1.1B), ether.fi Stake (-$620M), SSV (-$635M). These aren't niche protocols — this is the bedrock of Ethereum DeFi shedding billions. Price-driven, yes, but the magnitude signals capitulation-level outflows.

7. **Tron emerges as the stablecoin destination** — +$951M in stablecoin inflows is remarkable. Tron is absorbing capital that's leaving Ethereum, Arbitrum, and Avalanche. The payment/settlement use case is real and strengthening during risk-off periods.

8. **Arbitrum's slow bleed worsens** — -$296M stablecoin outflows (-6.3%), -$171M TVL decline (-8%), DEX volume -23%. Every metric is deteriorating for the leading L2. This is now a two-week trend that bears watching.

9. **Mantle's subsidy flywheel is working — for now** — +112% TVL backed by +$240M in real stablecoin inflows. The playbook: Aave V3 launches with 8M MNT + 1.5M GHO in incentives, Bybit's Mantle Vault funnels CEX users on-chain, and USDT0 migration provides clean rails. It's mercenary capital chasing yield, but at least it's real capital. The test comes when the 6-month incentive program expires (~August 2026).

10. **Berachain's growth is a mirage — for now** — +31% TVL looks impressive until you check stablecoin flows: -$7.4M (-6.3%). The entire move is BERA's 150% price rally (Feb 11 short squeeze after the 63.75M token unlock cleared). The "Bera Builds Businesses" strategic pivot and fresh Infrared staking yields are genuine catalysts, but new capital hasn't arrived yet. Watch whether stablecoin flows turn positive in coming weeks to confirm the narrative.
